# Contributing to Guru

We'd love to accept your patches and contributions to this project.

## Licensing

You (or your employer) retain the copyright to your contributions. Unless you
explicitly state otherwise, any contribution intentionally submitted for
inclusion in this repository shall be licensed under the repository's
[MIT License](LICENSE), without any additional terms or conditions.

## Submission Process

1. **Fork** the repository and create your branch from `main`.
2. **Implement** your changes.
3. Ensure your commits have descriptive messages and all source files have the
   appropriate license header.
4. **Submit a Pull Request** to the `main` branch.
